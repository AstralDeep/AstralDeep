## AstralDeep 1.9 (70)

- Automatic updates. The app checks once a day, verifies each update before installing it, and waits until you are not in the middle of a task before restarting. Check for Updates and Updates… live in the AstralDeep menu.
- Commands on your Mac. Approve a command once and read its real output; allow a program in a folder for the rest of the task.
- Astral browser pane. Astral can open pages, read them, fill forms and click controls in its own pane while you watch and take over at any time.
- One approval per task. Working on the same Mac no longer voids your approval, and Esc or Command-period cancels a turn.
- Graphite. A black, gray and purple default theme; your saved theme is unchanged.
- Quieter screens. Taglines and helper text are gone; controls stand on their own.
