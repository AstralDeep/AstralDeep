## Behavior

- Provider actions have distinct labels/routes and preserve readable choices, saved defaults, conditional fields and blank replacement credentials. Save validates and stores encrypted settings before acknowledging success; a subsequent bounded connection failure warns that the settings remain saved.
- Dashboard cards and filters follow server web roles with phone/tablet adaptation. Loading a prompt fills the composer without submitting it. Ordinary destinations, native Drafts and Advanced retain authenticated owner routing and truthful failure/retry states.
- Accepted server appearance repaints the shell and controls, exposes actual swatches and supports validated arbitrary colors. Rejected or canceled edits preserve the accepted palette.
- Settings requests and responses belong to the current surface, socket and generation. Delayed Save completion cannot dismiss another form or a newly reopened provider draft. Mandatory setup, private Work/Guidance, permission and credential boundaries remain in force.
- Native form pending states prevent repeated writes and retain edits through failures. First connection does not present a failed account load as confirmed empty content. Apple startup reads Keychain off the UI thread and discards restoration superseded by sign-out or a new sign-in; its final qualification is recorded separately.
